InkCore

From pen to performance.

InkCore is a pen-first music notation app for Android tablets: you write music by hand and it turns it into editable, playable notation you can carry to rehearsals and gigs. It also has tools for chord charts and lead sheets, including jazz.

Status: in testing on Android tablets.

Handwriting recognition is still being improved, so expect rough edges.

Your music stays yours

InkCore has no accounts and no ads, and it does not send your scores to us. The privacy policy explains exactly what the app does with files, the microphone and your Wi-Fi network.

Questions or problems
