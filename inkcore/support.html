Inkcore support

Inkcore is in testing on Android tablets, so bug reports are very welcome.

Get help

Email dan@mezyn.ski. Inkcore is a small project run by Daniel Mezynski, so replies can take a few days. Please write in plain text; there is no support phone line or chat.

Report a bug

The more of this you can include, the faster it can be fixed:

  1. What happened and what you expected instead.
  2. Steps to repeat it, starting from the library screen if you can.
  3. Your device: tablet model and Android version (Android Settings, About tablet).
  4. The Inkcore version (shown in Android Settings, Apps, Inkcore, usually near the bottom).
  5. Pen or finger: which stylus you used, if the problem is about writing or recognition.
  6. Whether it involved recording from the microphone, syncing with another device, importing a file, or printing.
  7. A screenshot or short screen recording if it helps. Crop out anything private.

Please do not send scores or recordings unless we ask. If a particular score is needed to see the problem, we will ask for it, and you can decide whether to send it.

We may also ask for the app’s diagnostic log. If so, we will tell you how to find it in the app. It lists what the app did and the names of scores you worked on, not their contents, so read it before sending. See the privacy policy for what it contains.

Common questions

The other device does not appear when I sync.
Both devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi network, and the other one needs “Make this device discoverable” switched on. Some networks block device discovery. In that case, type the other device’s address and port, shown on its screen, into “Sync manually”. Syncing is not encrypted, so use a network you trust; see the privacy policy.
Recording does not work.
Inkcore needs microphone permission. If you said no, turn it on in Android Settings, Apps, Inkcore, Permissions, then try again.
My purchase does not show as unlocked.
Open the unlock screen in the app and use “Restore purchase”. Payments, receipts and refunds are handled by Google Play, so for anything about payment contact Google Play support.
I deleted a score by mistake.
Look in “Recently deleted” in the library. A deleted score is kept there for a while before it is removed.
How do I remove my data?
See “Your choices and deleting your data” in the privacy policy.
